### 3. HORSE ELIGIBILITY AND ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

3.1 **Mandatory Requirements:** We accept a Horse for transport only if all of the following conditions are met:
* The animal is an active or registered racehorse (we do not transport breeding, sport, leisure, or non-racing equines);
* The Horse is microchipped and accompanied by a valid, original equine passport whose identification details exactly match the microchip;
* You are the legal owner of the Horse or hold explicit written authorization from the legal owner to arrange transport; and
* The transport is not conducted as part of a commercial sale, auction transfer, or change of legal ownership.

3.2 **Right of Refusal:** We reserve the absolute right to reject any booking request or refuse to load any Horse—prior to or at the point of pickup—if we determine that the Horse fails to meet the criteria in Section 3.1, exhibits signs of illness or unfitness to travel, poses a safety risk to crew or other horses, or is subject to an unresolved ownership or registration conflict.

### 8. TRANSIT OPERATIONS AND ANIMAL WELFARE

8.1 **Paramountcy of Animal Welfare:** The physical health, safety, and welfare of the Horse take absolute precedence over transit schedules. Wherever a conflict arises between schedule adherence and animal welfare laws or standards, welfare requirements prevail.

8.2 **Mandatory Driver and Equine Rest Rules:** In strict compliance with applicable transport and animal welfare regulations, our drivers observe mandatory driving limits (a maximum of 8 hours of continuous driving, followed by a minimum rest break of 45 minutes after every 4 to 4.5 hours of driving). Any journey exceeding 24 hours includes a mandatory 24-hour rest period during which the Horse is unloaded at an approved stabling facility. Scheduled transit durations incorporate these statutory rest periods, which cannot be waived or shortened.

8.3 **Welfare Monitoring and Live Tracking:** Our crew performs regular physical health and welfare checks at scheduled rest stops. Real-time GPS tracking is provided through the System for informational viewing only; temporary connectivity interruptions do not constitute a breach of contract, and continuous historical tracking logs are not guaranteed or retained for Client export.

8.4 **Estimated Arrival Times:** All transit durations and arrival times displayed on the System are good-faith operational estimates only and do not constitute guaranteed delivery deadlines.
